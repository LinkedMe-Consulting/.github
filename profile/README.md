## Executive Summary
LinkedMe Consulting is a South African technology firm specializing in high-scale cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and automated enterprise ecosystems. We bridge the gap between complex regional business requirements and world-class engineering standards.

## Core Specializations

### Cloud & Infrastructure
* **Cloud Native Migration**: Architecting scalable transitions to GCP and Hybrid environments.
* **Managed Operations**: SRE and Kubernetes orchestration for 99.9% uptime.
* **Google Workspace**: Enterprise-level modernization and security hardening.

### Artificial Intelligence & Data
* **Agentic Automation**: Developing autonomous workflows using Vertex AI and Gemini.
* **Data Engineering**: Robust ETL pipelines and BigQuery analytics for real-time insights.
* **Gemini Integrations**: Custom LLM implementation for internal business logic.
